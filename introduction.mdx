## Overview

Safe Start Child provides professional childproofing services to make your home a safe environment for babies and toddlers. You focus on enjoying parenthood while experts handle the safeguards. Prevention is key—our custom solutions prevent accidents, allowing little ones to explore freely.

According to organizations like Safe Kids Worldwide, millions of home injuries occur annually, but most are preventable. Safe Start Child tailors safety measures to your home's layout and your family's needs.

## Why Childproofing Matters

<Callout kind="alert">
  Every year, 3.5 million children in the US visit emergency rooms for home accidents. Simple safeguards like gates and latches make a big difference.
</Callout>

Childproofing protects curious explorers without restricting growth. Benefits include peace of mind for parents, reduced injury risks, and homes that grow with your family.
