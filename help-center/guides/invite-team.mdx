---
title: Inviting Your Team Members
description: Add staff or caregivers to your Safe Start Child organization.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team page">
    On your dashboard, click "Team" in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add new member">
    Click "Invite Member" and enter their email and role (e.g., Teacher, Admin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitation">
    Click "Send Invite". The team member will get an email to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>
