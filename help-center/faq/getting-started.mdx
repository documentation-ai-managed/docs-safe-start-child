## Overview

To start using Safe Start Child:

1. Visit `https://app.safestartchild.com`
2. Click "Sign Up" and enter your organization details
3. Verify your email address (check inbox)
4. Log in and complete your profile
5. Add your first project or student record

You're ready to use the Safe Start Child dashboard!
