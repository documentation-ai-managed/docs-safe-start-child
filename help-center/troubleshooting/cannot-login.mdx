---
title: Cannot Log In
description: Fix login problems on Safe Start Child.
---

## Symptoms

- You see a "Login failed" error
- Reset password email not received

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check email address">
    Make sure you enter the correct email used at signup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and check spam/junk folder for reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If still unable to log in, email support@safestartchild.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
