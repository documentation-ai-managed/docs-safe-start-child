## Prepare Your Home Information

Gather key details to make the assessment efficient.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Measure Key Areas" icon="ruler">
    Measure doorways, staircases, cabinet heights, and window openings. Note any custom furniture or unique layouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify Risks" icon="alert-triangle">
    Walk through your home and list potential hazards: cabinets, outlets, stairs, pools, or balconies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Layout" icon="camera">
    Take photos or sketch floor plans. Share these during booking for preliminary advice.
  </Step>
</Steps>
